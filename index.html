The Gap

Why culture fit
gets overlooked.

Most hiring tools stop at the resume. They tell you what a candidate has done — but say nothing about whether they'll thrive in your environment.

①
Credentials don't predict retention.

The #1 cause of early turnover isn't skill gaps — it's culture misalignment. Yet most ATS systems ignore it entirely.

②
Gut instinct isn't scalable.

Hiring managers sense fit in interviews — but by then you've already spent weeks narrowing a 400-applicant pool the wrong way.

③
Personality tests feel like homework.

Long-form assessments kill candidate experience and completion rates. There's a better way to gather the same signal.

The Process

Four layers. One ranked shortlist.

HireDepth's pipeline runs automatically — from raw applications to a prioritized, scored candidate list.

01

Resume Scan

AI parses every application — credentials, skills, job history, tenure patterns, and role relevance — in seconds.

02

Culture Match

You define your workplace culture type — collaborative, innovative, hierarchical, or results-driven — using our SHRM-aligned profiling framework.

03

Personality Fit

Candidates complete a lightweight 8-question animal archetype quiz. Results are mapped to behavioral traits required for each role.

04

Ranked Shortlist

HR receives a prioritized candidate list with composite fit scores across resume, culture, and personality — ready for first-round interviews.

Capabilities

Three engines. One decision.

Resume AI

Structured extraction of skills, experience depth, career trajectory, and role-specific signals — parsed from any resume format in under 3 seconds.

Core Differentiator

Culture Profiling

Based on established frameworks from SHRM and Indeed, HireDepth profiles your organization's culture across four primary types — and scores every candidate against it.

  • 4-quadrant culture typology
  • Team-level culture segmentation
  • Culture drift detection over time

Personality Matching

Inspired by animal archetype frameworks, our quiz maps candidates to behavioral profiles — wolf, dolphin, bear, eagle — and surfaces alignment with role requirements.

  • 8-question micro-assessment
  • 12 animal archetypes mapped
  • Role-archetype match matrix
